# Description (4000 characters)
DeepDesk turns a one-line research topic into an analyst-grade report. Idea validation, market sizing, competitor analysis, and background research for a proposal — work that used to take days is done in hours.

■ How it works
1. Write the topic and the angle you care about.
2. An AI research agent plans the research, then searches and cross-checks dozens to hundreds of web sources. Official statistics are used first when available.
3. Follow progress live in the app and receive the finished report in the app and by email.

■ What's in a report
Summary → key metric tiles and charts → detailed analysis → recommendations → the full list of sources consulted, so every claim can be traced.

■ Three sizes
• Light: 7–8 pages — quick validation
• Standard: 10–13 pages — with metric tiles and comparison tables
• Deep: 20+ pages — with charts and the complete source list

■ Made for
• Founders validating a business idea
• Anyone who needs background research for a plan, pitch or report
• Marketers and PMs tracking competitors and market trends
• Students and researchers doing literature groundwork

■ Good to know
• No account needed. We only ask for an email address to deliver the report.
• You pay per report through in-app purchase.
• AI-written content is a starting point — verify the sources before important decisions.
• Questions or issues: use "Report a problem" in the app or email support@lightonpluslab.com. If a report falls short, we revise it once for free.
